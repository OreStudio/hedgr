Sound effects in this directory are from Kenney's UI, interface and sci-fi packs, both
released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0). Attribution is not required, and
the credit below is kept because the packs ask for it.

  click1.wav            UI SFX Set, by Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl)
  confirmation_001.wav  Interface Sounds (1.0), by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
  confirmation_002.wav  Interface Sounds (1.0), by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
  error_001.wav         Interface Sounds (1.0), by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
  error_002.wav         Interface Sounds (1.0), by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
  bong_001.wav          Interface Sounds (1.0), by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
  intro_swell.wav       Sci-Fi Sounds (1.0), by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0:
                        spaceEngineLow_000 as the swell and
                        lowFrequency_explosion_000 as the impact, mixed with
                        ffmpeg (see tools/make_intro_sound.sh)

Downloaded from https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds (intro_swell.wav).
Mirrored at:
  https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-ui-audio
  https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds

The files are committed here rather than fetched at build time so the checkout
stays self-contained and no build depends on a network host.
